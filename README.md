# After the End: Neomoor Culture Revival

A Crusader Kings III submod for [After the End Fan Fork](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3192256710). It brings back the **Neomoor** culture and the **Shriner** faith from the old CK2 After the End: post-apocalyptic Florida, Orientalist fraternal orders, fezzes, parade elephants and all.

- **Steam Workshop:** [After the End Neomoor culture Revival](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3700777885) ([change notes](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/changelog/3700777885))
- **Game version:** CK3 1.19.*
- **Requires:** After the End Fan Fork

## What's in it

### Culture and faith
- **Neomoor culture:** a Floridian Islamic culture with its own name lists, traditions, coats of arms and title names along the Gulf Coast and the Atlantic seaboard.
- **The Shriner faith:** a post-apocalyptic Islamic fraternal order with its own doctrines, holy sites, traits and artifacts.
- **The Fez of Islam:** the Shriner holy order, on the map from the start with Captain Hasim at its head.
- **Embrace the Neomoor Ways:** decisions that let Sofloano rulers of the House of Nelson or the Shriner faith convert to Neomoor, each with its own lasting bonus.

### Event chains
- **The Prince Ali Parade:** a dynastic chain for the House of Nelson, parade elephant included.
- **Badroulbadour:** a romance chain for Shriner rulers on the way up.
- **Ibrahim the Mad:** a decadent chain inspired by the Ottoman sultan.
- **A Mummy on the Loose:** Ramses, recurring county trouble, curses and blessings.
- **The Sacred Breakfast** and **the Cafecito:** Shriner and Sofloano court ceremonies.
- **Ya-Shamal:** a once-per-game ceremony for the ruler who liberates Detroit under the Shrine.

### Systems
- **The Pilgrimage to Tampa:** a full Shriner pilgrimage through the Grand Bazaar. Encounters react to your ruler's personality, and it ends at the Imperial Potentate's temple.
- **The Pet Alligator:** a lifelong companion in the Stories panel, from a gator found in the swamp to a skull mounted in your hall.
- **The West Florida tug-of-war:** proclaim West Florida, assert Gulf independence, or annex it back.
- **The Florida Confederation:** Florida rises against Portia's Caribbean empire, with a Scramble for the Sunshine State.
- **The Lamp of Agrabah:** the faith's founding myth as a playable Legend told over five nights (needs the Legends of the Dead DLC).

## Roadmap

In progress: taking the Imperial Divan by diplomacy or force, crisis events with rival faiths and Potentates, more Neomoor and Nelson lore, Neomoor sub-cultures, and new game starts. Custom trait icons and event art will come later.

## Installation

Subscribe on the Steam Workshop and load it **after** After the End Fan Fork in the CK3 launcher playset.

To run it from this repo, clone it into `Documents/Paradox Interactive/Crusader Kings III/mod/` and add a matching `.mod` file that points to the folder.

## Development

The version lives in `package.json`. Running `npm version <patch|minor|major>` copies it into `descriptor.mod` through `scripts/sync-version.js`.
